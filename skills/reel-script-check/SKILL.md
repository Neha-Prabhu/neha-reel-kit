# Reel script check

Neha (Instagram: Neha Much) writes and shoots her own reels; edits are done later with neha-reel-kit. This skill works for any series or a one-off reel — don't assume a format. If she says the reel belongs to a series, follow that series' beats (ask once if you don't know them). Reels usually land at ~25–45 s after cutting.

## Voice
- Natural, human, like she's talking to one friend; a little funny, a little sassy. No AI-sounding phrases.
- Indian and relatable scenarios, not generic/foreign ones.
- Keep her voice — fix only what matters.

## Inputs
Her script (often dictated — rough is fine) + what the reel is about (a website link, product, place, idea). If there's a link, open it yourself to check claims (counts, "free", times) and spot the best visuals. Optional: series/format, visual ideas she already has.

## What to return (one message, short)
1. **Hook verdict** — does the first 1.5 s create a gap or contrast? Past data: big skip spike at 1–3 s when the hook is a slow setup. Give 2–3 alternative opening lines in her voice (relatable contrast works, e.g. "Pinterest DIYs are cute until they need 17 supplies"; a deliberate slip works too — "adopting a kid… I meant kitten" with a sticker pop for each).
2. **Tightened script** — changes marked inline. A good default shape: hook → one-line payoff → (series intro line, if it's part of a series) → what it is + 2–4 concrete examples she can point at → use-cases (each one a visual) → CTA / ending (for links: "you don't have to comment for the link, because here it is" → URL pops at the top). Flag factual errors and wrong episode numbers.
3. **Visual plan table** — one row per line: line · what's on screen (A-roll / screen recording / card pop / sticker cutaway / B-roll) · side (left/right column of the middle row, alternating) · notes (keep-bg or cutout, joke pops like a meme image for "I see the resemblance").
4. **Shot list** — A-roll tips: gesture to alternating sides (pops sit in the middle row, left or right column, away from her face); leave head-room for a text hook; hold objects where the pop will go. Screen recordings to capture: phone, slow steady scroll (it gets sped up), status bar and URL bar are cropped in the edit so she needn't hide them. Any B-roll.
5. **Asset checklist with exact file names** — original A-roll, `NN_<what>.png` per line, `_keep-bg` / `_cutout` suffixes (any photo can be a sticker — backgrounds are removed in the edit), listing/website screenshots (rebuilt as identical cards — just screenshot each one), a website screenshot for a link ending, wall colour/texture if any, caption font if she wants a special one, text hook words if any. Her exact words come from automatic transcription in the edit; she only confirms the lines flagged as uncertain.

Don't put a text hook into the plan unless she gives one; offer options only.
